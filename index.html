Paying us

Terms and policies

Terms of service

By giving us work or buying goods from us, you agree to these terms. We quote a price before starting any paid repair or installation. Government e-service fees are collected as fixed for Common Service Centres, and we are not responsible for decisions taken by a government department on an application. Goods carry only the warranty given by their manufacturer; we help you claim it. We do our best to keep documents and data safe while they are with us, but we cannot accept liability beyond the value of the work itself.

Privacy policy

We collect only what the work needs: your name, phone number, address and the documents you hand over for a specific application. We use them for that work alone. We do not sell or rent your information. Documents are returned to you, and copies we are required to keep are stored securely. Online payments are processed by Razorpay under its own privacy policy; card and bank details are never stored by us. If you want your contact details removed from our records, tell us and we will remove them, except where law requires us to keep a bill.

Refund and cancellation policy

If a payment fails but money leaves your account, tell us and we will check and refund the amount. An approved refund is sent back to the same account used for the payment, normally within five to seven working days. Government e-service fees cannot be refunded once an application has been submitted to the department. Goods may be returned within seven days if unused and in their original packing, with the bill. Work already carried out, such as printing, lamination or a completed repair, cannot be refunded. An order can be cancelled free of charge before the work begins.

Delivery of services

Counter work such as printing, lamination and photographs is handed over the same day. Online applications are submitted on the day the documents are complete; the department decides how long it then takes. CCTV installation is scheduled after the site survey, usually within three to seven days. Repairs are returned on the date given on your service ticket. We do not ship goods by courier; everything is collected from or installed by our shop.
